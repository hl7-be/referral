### Referral prescription data model

`BeModelReferralPrescription` is the logical data model behind the common referral prescription. It defines the information every referral prescription carries, independently of its FHIR representation, and is realized as the [`BeReferralServiceRequest`](StructureDefinition-be-referral-servicerequest.html) profile.

See the [Data models](datamodels.html) page for how this model relates to the FHIR resource and its specializations.
